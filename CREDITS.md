## Atkinson Hyperlegible font

By the Braille Institute of America, designed for low-vision readers. Regular and Bold, Latin
subset, in WOFF2, taken from the [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) package
`@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible` 5.3.0.

Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). The full text is in
`src/assets/fonts/OFL-AtkinsonHyperlegible.txt`.

- `fonts/AtkinsonHyperlegible-Regular.woff2`
- `fonts/AtkinsonHyperlegible-Bold.woff2`
- `fonts/OFL-AtkinsonHyperlegible.txt`

## Spoken prompts (English)

Generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py` with [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) and
the `en_US-ljspeech-medium` voice from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices (MIT), trained
on the [LJ Speech dataset](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/) (public domain). Piper (GPL-3.0) runs only through `uvx` to make the clips; it is not part of MindKeep.
Each clip says a prompt of `src/content/en/prompts.json`.

Licence: Public domain (LJ Speech recordings; voice model MIT)

- `audio/en/*.wav`
